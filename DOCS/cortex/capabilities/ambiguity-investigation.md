# Ambiguity Investigation Capability

Ambiguity investigation provides first-class workflows for inspecting unresolved or conflicting organizational interpretations.

## Ambiguity Types

- identity ambiguity,
- linkage ambiguity,
- temporal ordering ambiguity,
- semantic/ontology ambiguity,
- conflicting evidence ambiguity.

## Required Primitives

- explicit ambiguity objects,
- confidence decomposition,
- provenance chain visibility,
- ambiguity lifecycle states (open/reviewed/resolved/deferred),
- operator override/audit hooks.

## Validation Questions

- Can we isolate why a conclusion is uncertain?
- Can we review competing interpretations and evidence?
- Can ambiguity resolution be replayed and audited?
