# Decision Lineage Capability

Decision lineage reconstructs how decisions emerge, evolve, and influence outcomes.

## What Must Be Reconstructed

- initial decision context,
- tradeoff evolution,
- related discussions/artifacts,
- implementation consequences,
- reversals or superseding decisions.

## Required Primitives

- explicit decision objects in ontology,
- links to evidence/discussion/artifacts,
- temporal change history,
- confidence model for inferred decision links.

## Validation Anchor

For a major decision, Cortex should provide a navigable chain from origin context to operational outcomes with evidence-backed transitions.
