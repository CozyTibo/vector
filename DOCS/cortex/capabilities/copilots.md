# Copilot Capability Scenarios

Copilots are bounded cognition systems over Cortex memory, not generic assistants.

## Execution Copilot

- **What it understands:** execution status, blockers, ownership drift, dependency pressure.
- **Memory required:** raw evidence + canonical work graph + temporal transitions.
- **Continuity required:** actor/initiative linkage across tools.
- **Trust requirements:** every recommendation cites evidence and confidence.
- **Phases:** 01-09.

## Incident Copilot

- **What it understands:** incident chronology, decision chain, response coordination gaps.
- **Memory required:** message/event timeline, decision/action lineage.
- **Continuity required:** role shifts during incident windows.
- **Trust requirements:** clear separation between observed facts and inferred causes.
- **Phases:** 01-09.

## Onboarding Copilot

- **What it understands:** initiative history, design decisions, unresolved tradeoffs.
- **Memory required:** historical organizational memory and decision lineage.
- **Continuity required:** initiative and ownership evolution through time.
- **Trust requirements:** provenance-backed explanations with timeline context.
- **Phases:** 01-08.

## Dependency Copilot

- **What it understands:** fragile dependencies, blocker propagation, hidden coupling.
- **Memory required:** dependency graph + temporal change history.
- **Continuity required:** stable artifact and initiative identities.
- **Trust requirements:** expose uncertainty where links are inferred.
- **Phases:** 01-09.

## Review Copilot

- **What it understands:** implementation delta against prior decisions and architecture intent.
- **Memory required:** decision lineage, artifact history, replay comparisons.
- **Continuity required:** code/artifact lineage and decision references.
- **Trust requirements:** explicit evidence path for every flagged divergence.
- **Phases:** 01-09.

## Copilot Boundaries

- No autonomous policy decisions.
- No hidden reasoning claims without evidence references.
- No trust without provenance, ambiguity surfacing, and confidence semantics.
