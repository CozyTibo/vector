# Onboarding Intelligence Capability

Onboarding intelligence accelerates organizational understanding using replayable historical memory.

## Core Use Cases

- initiative reconstruction,
- architecture history understanding,
- decision lineage briefing,
- ownership evolution replay,
- historical incident context transfer.

## Why Replayability Matters

Onboarding depends on answers that remain auditable after ontology/model changes. Replayability ensures historical explanations can be regenerated and compared instead of becoming stale narratives.

## Why Provenance Matters

New team members need trusted context. Every explanation must be traceable to source evidence and confidence/ambiguity states.

## Required Architecture Primitives

- temporal memory slices,
- initiative/decision continuity,
- provenance-first retrieval,
- synthesis constrained by citations.

## Readiness Constraints

Without mature retrieval + synthesis guardrails, onboarding intelligence risks over-compression or overclaiming.
