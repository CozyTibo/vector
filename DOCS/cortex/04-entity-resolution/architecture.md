# Identity Resolution & Organizational Linking Layer Architecture

## Mission
Resolve continuity across canonical fragments and persist explainable cross-tool organizational linkages with temporal and provenance integrity.

## Owns
- identity matching across canonical objects,
- continuity reconstruction across time and tools,
- confidence-scored linkage records,
- ambiguity persistence for unresolved links,
- linkage replay/idempotency/versioning behavior.

## Does Not Own
- strategic reasoning,
- causal conclusion generation,
- executive summarization,
- hidden AI-only decisions.

## Input Contracts
- canonical events/entities/relations and their provenance,
- ontology and linkage policy definitions,
- version-pinned deterministic and AI-assisted linking rules.

## Output Contracts
- resolved identity links,
- organizational continuity records,
- ambiguity/conflict linkage records,
- confidence and provenance propagation metadata.

## Core Guarantees
- deterministic-first linking,
- bounded AI assistance only for unresolved ambiguity,
- replay-comparable linkage outputs,
- explainable and inspectable linkage decisions.
