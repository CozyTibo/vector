# Linkage Observability

## Required Metrics
- linkage throughput/latency,
- deterministic vs inferred linkage ratio,
- confidence distribution and drift,
- ambiguity volume and aging,
- conflict rate,
- replay divergence rate by linkage type.

## Required Alerts
- sudden confidence collapse,
- ambiguity explosion,
- provenance discontinuity in linkage records,
- replay divergence anomaly,
- cross-tool mismatch spike.

## Operator Views
- identity continuity health,
- initiative continuity health,
- ownership evolution stability,
- unresolved conflict queues.
