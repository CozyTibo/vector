# AI-Assisted Linking Model

## Allowed AI Scope
- semantic initiative matching hypotheses,
- ambiguous ownership interpretation hypotheses,
- cross-thread continuity hypotheses,
- implicit relationship candidate generation.

## Forbidden AI Scope
- cannot invent deterministic identity facts,
- cannot overwrite explicit deterministic links,
- cannot mutate or omit provenance evidence,
- cannot create hidden or non-inspectable links,
- cannot produce strategic conclusions.

## Invocation Preconditions
- deterministic linking completed first,
- ambiguity explicitly classified,
- bounded evidence packet with source refs and temporal context.

## Output Obligations
- `inference_type`,
- `confidence_score` and `confidence_band`,
- `ambiguity_reason` when unresolved,
- `supporting_evidence_refs`,
- `inference_version`, `inferred_at`, `replay_version`.

## Explainability Requirement
Every AI-assisted link must include operator-readable rationale summary and evidence references.
