# Identity Resolution Model

## Definition
Identity resolution determines whether multiple canonical references describe the same underlying organizational subject over time.

## Resolution Scope
- person continuity across tools,
- initiative continuity across artifacts/discussions,
- discussion continuity across channels/threads,
- concern continuity across events and artifacts.

## Model Characteristics
- continuity reconstruction, not semantic storytelling,
- evidence-grounded linking with confidence,
- temporal-aware and replay-safe.

## Output Types
- confirmed link,
- candidate link,
- conflicting link set,
- unresolved ambiguity record.
