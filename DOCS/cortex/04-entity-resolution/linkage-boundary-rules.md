# Linkage Boundary Rules

## Linkage Layer May
- resolve identity continuity from canonical evidence,
- create confidence-scoped organizational links,
- preserve ambiguity and conflicts explicitly.

## Linkage Layer May Not
- perform strategic reasoning,
- emit executive interpretations,
- hide AI assumptions or inferred links,
- mutate canonical/raw truth directly,
- silently rewrite historical continuity.

## Boundary Enforcement
- deterministic-first policy required,
- AI usage only for approved ambiguity scopes,
- all outputs must be explainable with provenance.
