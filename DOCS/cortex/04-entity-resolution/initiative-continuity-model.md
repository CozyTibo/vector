# Initiative Continuity Model

## Objective
Reconstruct long-running initiative continuity across tools and artifacts.

## Continuity Inputs
- discussions,
- artifacts,
- tickets/issues,
- pull requests,
- docs/channels,
- decision/action references.

## Continuity Strategy
- deterministic initiative anchors when explicit identifiers exist,
- inferred continuity candidates when explicit anchors absent,
- preserve competing initiative continuity hypotheses where ambiguous.

## Replay/Evolution
- initiative continuity is replay-reconstructable from canonical evidence sets and versioned linkage policies.
