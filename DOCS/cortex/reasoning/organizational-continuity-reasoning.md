**Status:** constitutional doctrine.

## Scope

## Rules

1. **Authoritative links** may justify causal edges only when `link_authority` and temporal validity gates pass.  
2. **Candidates / hints** never justify sole support for a causal edge — at most `uncertainty` or `weak_cross_system_bridge`.  
3. **OrgGraphProjectionV1** ingress may supply **structural** walk inputs; causal claims must still cite raw or org ledger lineage.
