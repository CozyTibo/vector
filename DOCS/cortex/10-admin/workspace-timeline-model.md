# Workspace Timeline Model

## Purpose
Provide chronological operational history of cognition infrastructure behavior and interventions.

## Timeline Events
- connector failures/recoveries,
- replay operations (start/progress/completion/divergence),
- extraction/ontology version changes,
- canonicalization migrations,
- corruption detections and containment actions,
- operator actions and approvals.

## Timeline Views
- global workspace timeline,
- phase-specific timeline,
- scope-filtered timeline (tenant/connector/replay-job/object).

## Timeline Utility
- incident reconstruction,
- trust trend analysis,
- regression and drift investigation.
