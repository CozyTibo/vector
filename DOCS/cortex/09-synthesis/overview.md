# Synthesis & Interaction Layer Overview

## Why This Phase Exists
This phase isolates a specific transformation responsibility in the Cortex cognition pipeline so downstream intelligence remains deterministic, replayable, and explainable.

## Responsibilities
- Narrative rendering over context packs
- Evidence citation and confidence framing
- Ambiguity-preserving output formatting

## Non-Responsibilities
- No fabrication beyond supplied evidence
- No hidden certainty escalation
- No bypass of retrieval boundaries

## Ownership Boundary
This phase owns only its contract outputs and may not bypass adjacent layers.
