# Phase 02 Trust Boundaries

## Core Rule
Phase 02 preserves evidence continuity.
It does not produce organizational intelligence.

## Guarantees
Phase 02 guarantees:
- preserved observed evidence continuity,
- preserved provenance continuity,
- preserved replay lineage continuity,
- preserved temporal continuity across captured revisions,
- deterministic replay boundaries over preserved scopes.

## Non-Guarantees
Phase 02 intentionally does not guarantee:
- complete provider omniscience,
- recovery of states never observed,
- semantic correctness of organizational conclusions,
- causal truth,
- identity/canonical truth.

## Replay Boundary
Replay-safe means preserved evidence can be replayed deterministically and non-mutatingly.
Replay-safe does not mean perfect recreation of objective provider reality forever.

## Reconstruction Boundary
Historical reconstruction means:
"what Cortex preserved and can prove for scope/time T."
It does not mean:
"everything that objectively happened."
