# Temporal/Causal Intelligence Layer Overview

## Why This Phase Exists
This phase isolates a specific transformation responsibility in the Cortex cognition pipeline so downstream intelligence remains deterministic, replayable, and explainable.

## Responsibilities
- Deterministic timeline reconstruction
- Causal hypothesis artifacts with evidence references
- Uncertainty-explicit inference records

## Non-Responsibilities
- No canonical fact rewriting
- No certainty claims without confidence support
- No connector-specific semantic logic

## Ownership Boundary
This phase owns only its contract outputs and may not bypass adjacent layers.
