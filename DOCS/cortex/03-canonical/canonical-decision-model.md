# Canonical Decision Model

## Purpose
Represent explicit or strongly evidenced choices with alternatives and consequences metadata.

## Lifecycle
- detected from deterministic cues where explicit decision markers exist,
- inferred candidate decisions from discussion evidence when allowed,
- superseded when later decisions replace prior ones.

## Ontology Semantics
- decisions relate to topics, artifacts, and projects.
- decisions are not strategic evaluation outputs.

## Provenance
- must include source evidence chain and rationale references.

## Temporal
- decision timeline supports proposed -> accepted -> superseded states.

## Ambiguity/Confidence
- inferred decisions require confidence and ambiguity rationale.
- unresolved candidate decisions remain non-authoritative.
